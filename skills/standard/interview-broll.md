# Standard — Interview and the customer's own pictures and clips

When there are several speakers or the customer added their own pictures or clips (with "where to use" notes):

1. **Never raw, unframed media**: frame the customer's screenshots, product photos and clips in one of these Standard containers over the grid or gradient ground:
   - **A solid browser or app window**: a crisp white or slate card (`border-radius: 22px`, 10 px padding, three traffic-light dots and a topic pill, a deep shadow), a slow 1.0 → 1.05 push inside, floating ✓ pills beside it.
   - **A 2×2 or strip portfolio grid** for several examples or results: framed thumbnail cards with ▶ badges and label pills.
   - **A split broadcast stage**: the speaker in a ● LIVE frame on one side, the customer's clip in a device or browser frame on the other.
2. **Speaker nameplates**: a solid lower third (`lower-third-kinetic`): a round avatar or icon badge, the bold name, a solid accent role pill.

## The customer's own pictures and clips
Where and how much of each file to show is judged as the shared files skill says; in Standard it sits in one of the containers
