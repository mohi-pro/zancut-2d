# Standard — Storytelling

Every scene is a custom Standard graphic (captions hidden), synthesized from the script with the making playbook's mechanics (M01–M20) and joined by its cutless transitions. No two neighbouring scenes share a layout silhouette.

## A 4-act shape (adapt it to the script)
1. **Hook and problem tension (first ~20%)**: M01, M05, M07, M09, M13. Open by dramatizing the hook (looping pain rows, a slashed myth or cost, audience questions, calendar sheets flipping). Turn into the body at about 4.5–5.5 s with a portal zoom or whip-pan.
2. **Solution, device showcase and contrast proof (20–55%)**: M02, M03, M04, M06, M08. Morph the problem into the hero hub, zoom into a living phone, browser or editor with its pills or tool dock; prove it with a ✕ vs ✓ split or an input → hub → output bridge.
3. **Roadmap and colour-block pattern interrupt (55–80%)**: M10, M11, M12, M20 + `stage-elevated-wash`. Show the steps or pillars; on the big pivot sentence (a `"pivot": true` row), a colour-block switch into full amber or full accent blue resets attention.
4. **Value, schedule and action (80–100%)**: M13, M14, M15, M16, M17. A stamped qualifier, a calendar and clock, the hero metric or offer card with a clicked button, or a comment box typing the call to action.

Music: none unless the customer gave their own track; the voice and the 4-pass sound carry the rhythm.

## Standard Storytelling rules for the scene file
- **The spoken key words are on screen** (captions are hidden): the scene's headline at the top (y 8–20%) with the emphasis word in the accent, #FACC15 or a tilted solid pill; on 9:16 reels, a dark rounded subtitle pill (#1E293B) at y 88% with the spoken clause.
- **Every join is cutless**: the shared playbook's join rule (whip-pan, portal zoom, optical swap, card reframe, wire thread, colour-block wipe, badge spin).
- **3–5 cues per scene** from the sound system.
