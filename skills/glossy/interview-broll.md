# GLOSSY + INTERVIEW & CUSTOMER FOOTAGE

Used for interview videos (two or more people talking, a podcast, a conversation filmed with one camera) and whenever the customer
added their own pictures or clips.

## Interview rules
- The people stay on screen most of the time (full frame); the speaker cut-out modes (pop-out box, behind the head, background
  replacement) are NOT used — several people in the picture break the cut-out.
- Use `lower-third-kinetic` to name each person the first time they speak (name + role from the customer's notes or the words) and
  for one-line takeaways; `over-torso-glass` for numbers, lists and proof; `full-broll-takeover` for the strongest metaphors and the
  customer's clips; `masked-pip-stage` for diagrams.
- Change mode on sentence boundaries; never the same mode twice in a row.

## The customer's own pictures and clips
- Show a picture or clip inside a glossy card (`"piece": "mine"` with `"mine": { "file", "kicker", "text" }`): a specular glass
  frame with a small kicker badge above (e.g. "CASE STUDY", "BEFORE") and an optional one-line caption — never stretched
  full-bleed, never cropped through a face. A logo sits on a clean glass plate.
